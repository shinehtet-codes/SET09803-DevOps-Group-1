# Code of Conduct

## 1. Purpose

This Code of Conduct establishes guidelines for respectful communication, professional behaviour, and effective collaboration among members of DevOps Group 1.

All team members are expected to follow these guidelines throughout the development of the SET09803 DevOps project.

## 2. Expected Behaviour

All team members should:

- Treat others with respect, fairness, and professionalism.
- Communicate clearly and constructively.
- Respect different opinions, ideas, and technical approaches.
- Complete assigned tasks responsibly and inform the team about any difficulties or delays.
- Support other members when technical problems arise.
- Participate actively in team discussions, meetings, and code reviews.

## 3. Unacceptable Behaviour

The following behaviours are not acceptable:

- Harassment, discrimination, bullying, or personal attacks.
- Disrespectful or offensive communication.
- Intentionally disrupting another member's work.
- Making unauthorized changes to another member's contributions.
- Taking credit for work completed by others.
- Ignoring agreed team procedures without communicating with the team.

## 4. GitHub Collaboration

To maintain a reliable and organized development process, team members must:

- Follow the agreed GitFlow branching strategy.
- Create feature branches from the latest `develop` branch.
- Use meaningful commit messages.
- Submit pull requests for changes to shared branches.
- Review code constructively and respectfully.
- Resolve merge conflicts through communication and cooperation.
- Avoid pushing directly to protected branches.

## 5. Responsibilities and Accountability

Each member is responsible for completing assigned tasks and maintaining the quality of their contributions.

The Scrum Master coordinates team activities, facilitates communication, and helps resolve development obstacles.

The Product Owner reviews completed work against the agreed requirements and acceptance criteria.

All developers are responsible for implementing, testing, and documenting their contributions.

## 6. Conflict Resolution

When disagreements or problems occur, team members should first attempt to resolve them through respectful discussion.

If an issue cannot be resolved, it should be raised with the Scrum Master and Product Owner for further discussion.

Serious or unresolved concerns may be referred to the module leader or appropriate university staff.

## 7. Enforcement

All team members are expected to follow this Code of Conduct.

Violations will be discussed and addressed fairly, depending on their severity. Repeated or serious violations may be escalated to the module leader.

## 8. Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project-related activities, including:

- GitHub repositories, issues, and pull requests.
- Team meetings and discussions.
- Discord and other communication platforms.
- Code reviews and collaborative development.
- Project documentation and submissions.

## 9. Acknowledgement

By participating in DevOps Group 1, all members agree to follow these guidelines and contribute to a respectful, productive, and collaborative development environment.
